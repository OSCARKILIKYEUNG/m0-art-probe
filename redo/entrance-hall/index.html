Drag anywhere on the left to move. Tap the floor to walk, or use WASD or the arrow keys. Tap Run to switch between running and walking, or hold R to run. C jumps. Shift rolls.

The oak doors lead to the Great Hall and Professor Dumbledore. The west arch leads to the Grand Staircase. Climb the stairs along the wall to the waiting terrace. The moving flight visits two upper landings in turn. Step aboard while it is still. Stay on the steps to ride to the other landing, or wait on the stone platform for its return.

J fires Basic Cast. Use 1 or Space for Flipendo, 2 for Wingardium Leviosa and 3 for Lumos. Drag an attack or a directional spell to aim. Q uses Ultimate. Six Basic Cast impacts refill its ring.
